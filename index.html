Project

Espejo

“Espejo” grew out of a familiar habit: constantly checking myself in the mirror for flaws and abusing filters to meet other people's beauty standards. The piece aims to insert itself into the tool used to criticize ourselves — the mirror. Installed as an unremarkable mirror in an unremarkable room, it waits for the same instinctive glance most mirrors invite — the one that usually goes looking for flaws — and answers it with a chosen compliment instead, rendered directly onto the person standing in front of it.
